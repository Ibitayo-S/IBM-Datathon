Extra transfer compares certificate-message size with the classical reference. It assumes the measured download rate in both directions and excludes cryptographic processing, packet loss, round trips and protocol overhead.

What the model predicts

The next authentication-exchange delay under an unchanged cryptographic configuration, using recorded simulation data.

Network request duration and download rate are measured separately. They are not inputs to this trained model. The transfer estimate is extra certificate-message bytes × 8 ÷ measured bits per second.
